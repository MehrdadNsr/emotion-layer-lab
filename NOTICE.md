# Notices and attribution

- **Research basis.** The 171 emotion concepts, and every finding marked as coming from the paper, are from N. Sofroniew et al., "Emotion Concepts and their Function in a Large Language Model", Anthropic, April 2026 (arXiv:2604.07729). The experiments were run on Claude Sonnet 4.5. Paper findings are paraphrased and cited, not copied.
- **Emotion list.** The list of 171 emotion labels was taken from the dataset card of `ryancodrai/emotion-probes` on Hugging Face (CC BY 4.0). Changes: 10 emotions were added from psychology (marked "added" with evidence level D), and every emotion was given a category, family, policy, intensity ladder, appraisal and examples, which are this project's own work.
- **Ideas from other work.** Display rules follow Ekman and Friesen's concept from psychology. The local model's appraisal step follows the chain-of-emotion idea of Croissant et al., PLOS ONE (2024). Both are cited as inspiration; no text or code was copied.
- **No affiliation.** This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
